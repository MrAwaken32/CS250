CS 250 – Software Development Lifecycle

Sprint Review and Retrospective

This repository contains my Sprint Review and Retrospective from CS 250 at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout this course, I explored Agile and Scrum practices and learned how software development teams collaborate, communicate, and adapt to changing requirements.

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by first understanding what the user expects from the software and what problems the program should solve. Communication with stakeholders and gathering requirements are important steps in making sure the final product meets expectations. Creating user stories helps break down larger requirements into smaller, manageable tasks from the user’s perspective. Throughout CS 250, I learned how user stories help developers understand what features need to be created and why they are important. They also help the development team prioritize tasks and make adjustments based on feedback.

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

When developing programs, I prefer to start by understanding the requirements, planning the necessary steps, and breaking the work into smaller sections. I believe this makes development more organized and helps identify problems before they become more difficult to fix. Through this course, I learned the importance of Agile practices such as sprint planning, daily Scrum meetings, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. In my future development work, I hope to incorporate these practices to improve communication, track progress, and continuously improve my work. I also believe regularly testing software and gathering feedback are important for delivering a reliable product.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating effectively, being dependable, and working toward a common goal. Each team member has different responsibilities, but everyone contributes to the success of the project. I believe it is important to listen to others, respect different perspectives, and be willing to help when challenges arise. Throughout CS 250, I learned that Agile teams depend on collaboration, transparency, and accountability. Being open to constructive feedback and adapting to changes are also important qualities. In my future career, I want to continue developing these skills so I can contribute positively to a software development team.
